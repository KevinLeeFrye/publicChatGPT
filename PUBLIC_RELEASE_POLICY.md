# Kevin Frye Unlimited Public Release Boundary

This repository is the public front door and experiment yard for **Kevin Frye Unlimited** and **F.R.Y.E. Unlimited Studios**.

## Public by design

The following material may be published here when it is deliberately prepared for public inspection:

- browser-safe demonstrations and vertical slices;
- architecture summaries and non-secret specifications;
- verification summaries, reproducible test fixtures, and public evidence;
- screenshots, videos, art studies, and selected design documents;
- release packages explicitly approved for public distribution;
- issues, experiments, failures, and lessons that help the work grow.

## Protected by default

The following material must not be copied into this public repository unless Kevin explicitly releases it:

- credentials, provider keys, tokens, passwords, private URLs, or personal data;
- private source repositories and complete unreleased engines;
- legal, financial, investor, partner, or customer material;
- proprietary assets, licensed content, or third-party confidential files;
- unreviewed archives copied wholesale from private workspaces;
- claims of verification without matching logs, hashes, or reproducible evidence.

## Studio rule

**Build openly. Release intentionally. Verify before claiming. Preserve lineage.**

Public experiments should be descendants or exports. They should not overwrite the private source of truth.
